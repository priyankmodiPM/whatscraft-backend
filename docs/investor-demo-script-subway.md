Let's see this in action — with a QSR chain, on the single busiest week of the year, when every outlet needs new creative at once.

Picture Amit — runs a Subway near a college and IT park. It's Finals Week: exams, cricket, hungry students. HQ sent every outlet the same generic offer. But Amit knows his crowd wants a combo, not a solo sub.

**[Start demo]**

HQ's Finals Week banner is already sitting in his WhatsApp — 20% off any 6-inch sub. He could send it as-is. Instead, he taps Edit — [tap] — and just describes what he wants: a combo, for his crowd.

**[Bot starts generating — fill the wait]**

That's Adobe Express, building it live. No design brief, no agency back-and-forth, no waiting on HQ's queue in the one week every outlet needs assets fast.

**[Combo lands]**

Chicken Tikka Sub, cookie, cold drink — a real combo. Now watch — this isn't just image editing.

**[Pricing beat — slow down]**

The bot volunteers something: _"Separately these run ₹350. Nearby outlets running this combo are converting best at ₹250."_ That's chain-wide performance data, surfaced to one store owner, mid-decision. Amit taps Yes — [tap] — ₹250, struck-through pricing and all, updated automatically.

**[Key beat — the guardrail]**

Amit tries to call it "gluten-free" — the gym crowd loves that. The bot pushes back: _"Can't — the bread has wheat, that's a false claim."_ But it doesn't just say no — it offers three compliant ways to still win that crowd. Amit picks the gluten-free base — [tap] — and the bot rebuilds it, then adds a caveat most humans would miss: _"the cookie still has gluten, so I can't tag the whole combo."_ That's brand and compliance risk, caught automatically.

**[Final hook + send]**

One more edit — urgency, "first 20 guests, extra 10% off" — with compliant fine print written in, unasked. Then: WhatsApp to his opted-in lists, Instagram as a post and a Story, or both — [tap] — sent, from a single chat.

**[Close]**

Amit never opened a design tool, never waited on HQ, never risked a compliance slip. He typed like he talks — Hindi, English, both — and got a campaign that's local, fast, and still on-brand. Multiply Amit by every outlet, every festival, every brand. _That's_ WhatsCraft.
